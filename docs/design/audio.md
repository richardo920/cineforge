# Audio proposal

Keep dialogue, ambience, Foley/effects, and music as separate stems with start/duration cues on the picture timeline. Retain source, rights, speaker/character revision, and generation provenance for every cue.

Analyze speech alignment, loudness, true peak, clipping, silence, and sync; optionally use an audio-capable model for qualitative feedback. Technical checks cannot establish artistic quality: require human listening approval of intelligibility, music balance, transitions, and final mix. No generation, analysis, or mixing is implemented in the scaffold.
